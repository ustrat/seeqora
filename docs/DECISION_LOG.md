| 2026-11-10 | Time words in questions are read by rules in the core (`when.rs`), and they reorder search; they never filter it | Decided | Owner saw "next appointment" answered with years-old reminders. Ranking was by words alone, and "next" meant nothing. Windows ("last Thursday", "next week", "in November", "yesterday") rank their items first. "next"/"upcoming" puts strong matches about today or later first, soonest first. A reminder counts by the date it mentions ("on 06/05"), not the day it was sent. "last"/"latest" puts strong matches newest first. Nothing is dropped, so a wrong reading costs rank, not the answer. The prompt marks each source past, today or upcoming. Same code for the results list, answers and the eval (T6). The golden set gains three next/last questions and one reminder fixture |
| 2026-11-10 | Trial Qwen3.5 4B Instruct (Q4_K_M, 3.0 GB, Apache-2.0) as the answer model | Decided: missed, superseded below | Owner asked. Expected to reason better, dates included, but about 2.7× the compute of Qwen2.5 1.5B. The loader now accepts `qwen3`/`qwen35` GGUFs and turns thinking off with the template's own empty `<think></think>` block; any reasoning the model still writes is hidden. Same bar on the S24 Ultra: ~1,200 tokens read in 15 s or less, writing at 5 tok/s or more. If it misses, try a smaller Qwen3.5 or keep Qwen2.5 1.5B. **Measured 2026-11-10, S24 Ultra:** 735 tokens read in 15.0 s (49 tok/s, ~24 s at 1,200), writing 9.5 tok/s, 5.7 GB peak. **Misses** on reading, and the memory rules out 8 GB phones |
